Ten car and light-truck steering-defect recalls were filed in the US between January 1 and October 8, 2026. That is the fewest of any year since 2020, and well under half the 25 filed by this point in 2024. Yet those ten campaigns cover 1,584,424 vehicles, because three of them account for 98% of that total. Steering recalls are getting rarer and bigger at the same time, and that is why 2026 reads like a wave when the filing count says the opposite.

Key facts

  • Load-bearing count: 10 car and light-truck steering recall campaigns filed Jan 1 to Oct 8, 2026 (strict definition: component label begins "STEERING"). Eleven under the broad definition, which adds one Lincoln driver-assist campaign.
  • This is the lowest, not the highest. Ten is below every year back to 2020 (2020: 12, 2024 peak: 25). The finding survives on every basis we tested, including the unrestricted one.
  • The unrestricted number, printed beside it so nothing is hidden: counting every vehicle type in NHTSA's file, including motorcycles, heavy trucks, buses and fire apparatus, 2026 returns 20 campaigns and 1,662,335 vehicles (21 campaigns and 1,663,348 vehicles on the broad label test). Restricting to cars and light trucks nearly halves the count and barely touches the units.
  • Vehicle census, an attributed total and never a ranking: 1,584,424 vehicles across the 10 car campaigns. These are manufacturers' launch-time estimates of units potentially affected.
  • Three campaigns are 98% of those vehicles: Jeep 26V363000 (1,076,999), Ram 26V621000 (265,512), VW/Audi 26V590000 (208,724 US). The other seven campaigns add 33,189 between them.
  • Jeep 26V363000 (filed 2026-06-04): 2021-2025 Wrangler, Wrangler 4xe, Gladiator. Filed under a hydraulic power-steering pump label; the consequence NHTSA records is a vehicle fire, and its remedy text tells owners to park outside. Followed investigation PE24024. Owner letters expected 2026-07-09.
  • Ram 26V621000 (filed 2026-09-29): 2022-2026 Ram ProMaster vans plus the 2024-2026 Ram 3500 EV. Water can enter the electric power-steering wiring harness and disable assist. Interim owner letters expected 2026-11-17; VINs searchable at NHTSA from 2026-10-06.
  • VW/Audi 26V590000 (filed 2026-09-11): 2018 VW Tiguan, 2018-2019 VW Atlas, 2019-2021 Audi Q3. A corroded right-side steering rack mounting bolt can break, risking loss of steering control. Owner letters expected 2026-11-10.

How many cars were recalled for steering problems in 2026, and is it really a "wave"?

Ten, on the measure that actually tracks how often defects get filed. We counted 10 car and light-truck steering-defect recall campaigns through October 8, 2026, or 11 under the broader label test. That is the lowest January-to-October total of the last seven years. The headlines read like a wave because a few of 2026's campaigns are enormous, not because more of them are being filed.

On the unrestricted basis, 10 of 2026's 21 steering-labelled campaigns are not cars or light trucks at all: two Honda motorcycle recalls and one Harley-Davidson, Kenworth and Peterbilt, Freightliner and Mack and Volvo heavy trucks, a Pierce fire-apparatus campaign, and two commercial step-van builders. It contains the word "steering" and is not a steering defect, which is why we report both counts instead of blurring them.

What a VIN check can and can't tell you here

A VIN check is a screening and history tool, not a repair tracker. It will flag that a steering recall is attached to a given vehicle, the same recall universe as NHTSA's free lookup, and beyond that show the accident, salvage and ownership history NHTSA's tool does not. What it cannot do is confirm the recall was fixed on that specific car, and it will not surface open NHTSA investigations such as PE26002, the ProMaster steering probe that preceded the Ram recall.

Method

Source file. NHTSA's Office of Defects Investigation recalls flat file, FLAT_RCL_POST_2010.txt, inside FLAT_RCL_POST_2010.zip. Downloaded 2026-10-08 at 07:30 UTC; the server reported Last-Modified: Wed, 07 Oct 2026 12:12:16 GMT; the archive's CRC was verified intact before parsing. The file carries 245,765 rows resolving to 15,297 distinct recall campaigns, and its newest report-received date is 2026-10-05.

Universe and filter. The flat file stores one row per campaign-and-model combination, while POTAFF (units potentially affected) and RCDATE (report received) are campaign-level fields repeated on every row. We therefore collapse to one record per CAMPNO first; counting rows would multiply-count every campaign by its model count. We keep RCLTYPECD='V' (vehicle recalls), filter RCDATE to January 1 through October 8 of each year from 2020 to 2026, and select campaigns whose component label COMPNAME begins with STEERING (strict, N = 10 for 2026) or merely contains it (broad, N = 11). Counts are distinct CAMPNO values.

The car and light-truck restriction. RCLTYPECD='V' means "vehicle", not "car": it carries motorcycles, buses, heavy trucks, fire apparatus and commercial chassis. Because this is a used-car page, the headline basis excludes them. Classification works in two passes: Part 573 filers that build no passenger vehicles are dropped whole (Harley-Davidson, Mack, Volvo Trucks, Paccar, Daimler Trucks, Pierce, Blue Bird, Gillig and the trailer, RV and fire-apparatus builders), and inside filers that build both, individual motorcycle and medium/heavy chassis model strings are dropped (Honda CMX300, CMX500, SCL500; BMW M 1000 R, M 1000 XR; Ford F-650 SD, F-750 SD, F-53, F-59; GM ZEVO; Ram 4500, 5500). The resulting per-campaign call ships as a vehicle_class column in the frozen CSV, so a reader can redo or reject it. Two borderline calls are flagged there and included: the Chevrolet Express and GMC Savana vans, and the Ram ProMaster vans, which are class 2-3 light commercial vehicles.

Aggregation. Units are the sum of POTAFF over distinct campaigns. The make leaderboard is grouped on the Part 573 filer (MFGTXT), not the make name (MAKETXT), because one campaign routinely spans several makes: 26V345000 and 26V399000 each list both Chevrolet and GMC, and a make-level count reports one General Motors campaign as two filers.

Robustness checks, reported either way.

  1. The restriction nearly halves the count and barely moves the units: 20 campaigns to 10 (-50%), 1,662,335 vehicles to 1,584,424 (-4.7%). Two statistics from one filter degraded in opposite directions, so both bases are published.
  2. The finding survives every basis: 2026 is the minimum of 2020-2026 on car-strict (10), car-broad (11), all-vehicle-strict (20), all-vehicle-broad (21), and on a deliberately generous union of label-matched plus defect-text-matched campaigns (28 for 2026, against 38 in 2025 and 81 in 2024). That last test exists because a label-based count would be confounded if NHTSA's labelling practice had drifted; it has not drifted enough to reverse the result.
  3. Label multiplicity is not the explanation. Only 4 of the 21 steering-labelled 2026 campaigns carry more than one component label, and in 3 of those both labels are steering. Only 26V242000 pairs steering with suspension.
  4. The make leaderboard reverses under the restriction, and the unrestricted version is the wrong one for this page: Honda's two campaigns are both motorcycle recalls.
  5. The window is three days short. The file's newest report-received date is 2026-10-05, not 2026-10-08, while comparison years are complete. At the measured 2026 rate of one car steering campaign every 28 days, three days cannot close the two-campaign gap to 2020 and 2025; it would take two late filings to produce a tie, and none to produce a reversal.

Corroboration. The four load-bearing campaigns (26V363000, 26V621000, 26V590000, 26V377000) were re-queried against api.nhtsa.gov/recalls/campaignNumber and matched the flat file exactly on component label, unit count and report-received date, 4 of 4. Owner-letter dates, remedy text and the October 6 VIN-searchability date are quoted from NHTSA's own Remedy field rather than from press coverage. Investigation PE26002's opening resume was read directly and names 2022-2023 Ram ProMasters, loss of power steering assist from water intrusion into the power steering module connector, 136,666 units estimated, opened 2026-03-02.

Known limits.

  • POTAFF is the manufacturer's launch-time estimate of units potentially affected. It is not a count of cars still on the road, still unrepaired, or still in the US. This file can support "how much exposure was opened" and cannot support "how many dangerous cars are out there" or any model-level "worst car" ranking.
  • The flat file has no repair-completion field, so nothing here measures how many of these vehicles were actually fixed.
  • The component label is NHTSA's categorisation, not a diagnosis. A campaign labelled steering can have a fire as its recorded consequence, as 26V363000 does.
  • The car and light-truck classification is ours and is a judgement at the class 2-3 van boundary. The vehicle_class column exists so it can be rejected.

Frequently asked questions

How many cars were recalled for steering defects in 2026, and is that a lot? Ten. As of October 8, 2026, NHTSA's own recalls file shows 10 car and light-truck steering-defect recall campaigns filed this year, covering 1,584,424 vehicles. That is the fewest of any year since 2020, and well under half the 2024 figure of 25. Counting every vehicle type in the file, including motorcycles, heavy trucks and buses, the total is 20 campaigns and 1,662,335 vehicles, rising to 21 and 1,663,348 on the broad label test. The vehicle count is high only because three campaigns are unusually large.

Which 2026 models were recalled for steering problems? The three largest, which together are 98% of the vehicles: 2021-2025 Jeep Wrangler, Wrangler 4xe and Gladiator (26V363000, 1,076,999); 2022-2026 Ram ProMaster vans and the 2024-2026 Ram 3500 EV (26V621000, 265,512); and the 2018 VW Tiguan, 2018-2019 VW Atlas and 2019-2021 Audi Q3 (26V590000, 208,724 US). The other seven are small: Hyundai Kona, a Roush-modified Nissan Frontier, two Chevrolet Express and GMC Savana van campaigns, a BMW X3, a 2016-2017 Range Rover and a Toyota Grand Highlander, Toyota TX and Lexus RX campaign. Note that six of the ten campaigns cover model-year 2025-2026 vehicles that are still effectively new; only the Jeep (2021-2025), the VW and Audi (2018-2021) and the Range Rover (2016-2017) reach genuinely used stock, and those three hold 81.1% of the vehicles. Enter a specific VIN at NHTSA to confirm whether that exact vehicle is included.

Does a VIN check tell me whether a steering recall was actually repaired? No. NHTSA's recalls flat file has no repair-completion field, and a VIN check surfaces that a recall exists on a vehicle, not whether it was fixed. Only NHTSA's own VIN lookup or a franchised dealer shows open-versus-completed status for a specific VIN.

Is the Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator power-steering recall a fire risk? Yes. Campaign 26V363000 is filed under a hydraulic power-steering pump label, but the consequence NHTSA records is a vehicle fire: the pump wiring can overheat and ignite. NHTSA's remedy text advises owners to park outside and away from structures until the repair is done. It is the single largest 2026 steering-labelled campaign, 1,076,999 vehicles, and it followed investigation PE24024.

Does the Ram ProMaster power-steering recall affect pickups? No. Campaign 26V621000 covers 2022-2026 Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 ProMaster vans plus the 2024-2026 Ram 3500 Electric, 265,512 vehicles in total, not the Ram heavy-duty pickups. NHTSA's summary says water may enter the electric power-steering wiring harness and disable power-steering assist. It was filed 2026-09-29, interim owner letters are expected to be mailed November 17, 2026, and the VINs became searchable on NHTSA.gov on October 6, 2026.

Is my VW Tiguan, VW Atlas or Audi Q3 part of the steering-rack recall? Possibly. US campaign 26V590000 covers the 2018 VW Tiguan, 2018-2019 VW Atlas and 2019-2021 Audi Q3, 208,724 US vehicles. NHTSA records the consequence as a broken steering rack housing causing a loss of steering control, and the remedy as replacing the right-side steering rack mounting bolt, with owner letters expected November 10, 2026. Larger figures in international coverage are worldwide totals and are not the US count. Check your VIN at NHTSA.

Which carmaker filed the most steering recalls in 2026? Nobody stands out, and the flatness is the honest answer. On a car and light-truck basis two groups filed two campaigns each, General Motors (the Chevrolet Express and GMC Savana vans) and Stellantis (Jeep and Ram), and six other filers each filed one. A ranking that puts Honda on two is counting its two motorcycle recalls, which do not belong in a used-car comparison.

What does a STEERING recall label actually mean on NHTSA's site? It is the component category NHTSA assigns a safety recall under 49 CFR Part 573, covering parts from the rack, column and linkages to electric and hydraulic power-assist systems. The label marks the system involved; the actual hazard, whether loss of assist, loss of control or fire, is spelled out in the campaign's own defect and consequence text, which is why reading the specific campaign matters more than the one-word category.

How is a recall different from an NHTSA investigation like the ProMaster probe? A recall is a manufacturer's formal defect report with a remedy attached; an investigation is NHTSA examining whether a defect exists. PE26002, opened 2026-03-02 on 2022-2023 Ram ProMasters for loss of power steering assist from water intrusion, is the probe that preceded the Ram recall. Investigations carry no VIN list and do not appear in a VIN recall check, so track them at NHTSA rather than through a history report.
