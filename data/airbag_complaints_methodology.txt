METHODOLOGY — Used cars with the most airbag complaints (NHTSA ODI)

DATASET: NHTSA Office of Defects Investigation (ODI) Consumer Complaints flat file.
FILE: FLAT_CMPL.txt (tab-delimited), from https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/ffdd/cmpl/FLAT_CMPL.zip
FILE POSTED/DATED BY NHTSA: 2026-09-06
RETRIEVED & PROCESSED: 2026-09-08
TOTAL COMPLAINT RECORDS IN FILE: 2,243,124

AIRBAG FILTER: records whose component field (COMPDESC, field 12) contains the
string "AIR BAGS". This captures "AIR BAGS", "AIR BAGS:FRONTAL",
"AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW", sensor/occupant-classification, inflator-module, clockspring,
and the "ALLEGED COUNTERFEIT AIR BAG" subtype.
AIRBAG COMPLAINTS MATCHED: 156,619 across 1,873 distinct make/model combinations.

FAILURE-TYPE CLASSIFICATION: regex applied to the free-text complaint narrative
(CDESCR, field 20), uppercased, in priority order:
  1. non_deploy  — a negation/failure token ("did not / didn't / failed to / never /
     none of / would not") bound to a deploy/activate/go-off/inflate token
     (the scariest failure: airbag did NOT deploy in a crash).
  2. inadvertent — inadvertent/unexpected/spontaneous/for-no-reason/while-driving deployment.
  3. warn_light  — airbag/SRS warning light or lamp on/illuminated.
  4. recall_process — mentions Takata or a recall (parts-wait, remedy-delay complaints)
     but none of the failure modes above.
  5. other — none of the above.
Priority means a narrative describing multiple issues is assigned to the most severe
matched category. non_deploy outranks warning-light and recall mentions.

OVERALL SPLIT (n=156,619):
  recall_process 54,659 (34.9%)
  non_deploy     34,664 (22.1%)
  warn_light     33,359 (21.3%)
  other          27,353 (17.5%)
  inadvertent     6,584  (4.2%)

HARM (self-reported by complainants, DEATHS field 11 / INJURED field 10):
  Deaths 960, Injuries 40,323 — after excluding two obvious data-entry errors
  (a 2006 Honda Pilot complaint listing 99 deaths and a 2005 Toyota Highlander
  complaint listing 45 deaths for a single-driver incident). Uncleaned death sum: 1,104.

NORMALIZATION / ROBUSTNESS CHECK: raw complaint volume favors high-population
best-sellers, so we also report the WITHIN-MODEL SHARE of complaints that are
non-deployment. Because the denominator is each model's own airbag-complaint total,
this share is fleet-size neutral and it REVERSES the raw leaderboard (see article).
A literal complaints-per-100,000-registered-vehicles rate would require proprietary
registration counts (S&P Global Mobility / Experian) that are not freely available;
we did not estimate or fabricate them. The within-model non-deployment share is our
fleet-neutral metric.

OUTPUT: airbag_complaints_by_model.csv — one row per make/model with total airbag
complaints, non_deploy count and %, inadvertent, warning_light, recall_process, other,
non-deployment-in-a-crash count (CRASH flag = Y), and self-reported deaths.
